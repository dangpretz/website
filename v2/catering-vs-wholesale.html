CATERING VS WHOLESALE FAQ

What’s the difference between catering and wholesale?

Catering is pretzel boxes, bomb trays, and dips for a single event — weddings, corporate lunches, game days. Wholesale is standing or on-demand frozen cases for a restaurant, bar, office, grocery, or venue that stocks its own service. Same Salt Lake City kitchen. Different job.

Which page should I use?

One event: the catering page — order online, or send a custom catering inquiry. FAQ recommends at least 48 hours for standard catering, and a week for events of 50+ people. Delivery for catering is offered in the SLC metro. Standing inventory: the wholesale inquiry on this page. Existing wholesale partners order at the portal.

Is a corporate lunch wholesale?

No. A one-off corporate lunch or campus event is catering. Offices and campuses can buy wholesale when they want standing orders and no kitchen build-out — frozen cases they reheat. Use catering for the lunch. Use wholesale for the account. More on office wholesale.
