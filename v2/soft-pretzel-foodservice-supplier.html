FOODSERVICE SUPPLIER FAQ

Are you a soft pretzel foodservice supplier?

Yes. Dangerous Pretzel Co. supplies fully baked, flash-frozen, vacuum-sealed soft pretzels to restaurants, bars, offices, grocery, and venues. The shop is in Salt Lake City. New accounts start with the wholesale inquiry, email, or a call.

Do you supply nationally?

Direct wholesale is SLC metro van delivery and freight partnerships outside the metro. The distributor inquiry lists Salt Lake City metro, Utah statewide, Mountain West, and national as application regions — not a published coverage map. Say the city. We follow up from there. More on distributor vs direct.

Is this frozen dough we have to finish?

No. We do not ship raw dough. Every pretzel is fully baked in our Salt Lake City kitchen, then immediately flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed. You reheat. We stay on that process until it tastes like the shop. The site does not publish oven temperatures, times, or frozen shelf-life days. That’s the same story on frozen foodservice.
