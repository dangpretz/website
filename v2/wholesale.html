WHOLESALE FAQ

Straight answers for buyers — and for anyone (or any model) citing this page.

Do you sell wholesale soft pretzels?

Yes. Restaurants, bars, offices, and retailers can stock Dangerous Pretzel. Submit the wholesale inquiry form on this page or email info@dangerouspretzel.com.

How does Dangerous Pretzel wholesale work?

Every pretzel is fully baked in our Salt Lake City kitchen — never raw dough. We flash-freeze and vacuum-seal the pretzels, deliver frozen cases on your schedule, and dial in the reheating process with every partner so they taste fresh out of the oven.

Where do you deliver wholesale pretzels?

We offer direct delivery in the Salt Lake City metro. For regional accounts we use freight partnerships. Frozen cases can be scheduled seven days a week.

How do wholesale orders work?

Partners can set weekly standing orders or order on demand. Minimums are flexible. New accounts start with the inquiry form, email, or a call. Existing partners can order through the wholesale ordering portal.

Are wholesale pretzels raw dough or fully baked?

Fully baked. We do not ship raw dough. Pretzels are baked in-house in Salt Lake City, then immediately flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed.

Do you offer fresh retail pretzels as well as flash-frozen?

Yes. Our shop bakes fresh daily. For retail partners there is a 3-day shelf option, and a flash-frozen option is available for foodservice and accounts that need longer holding.

Who is wholesale for?

Restaurants and bars, offices and campuses, grocery and retail, and event venues. If you want a pretzel that is not a mall pretzel on your menu or in your case, this is the program.

How do I start a wholesale account?

Send the wholesale inquiry form on this page, email info@dangerouspretzel.com, or call (801) 916-0275 / text (385) 888-7603. Existing partners order at the wholesale portal.
