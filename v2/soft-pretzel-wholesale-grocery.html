TWO HOLDS. SAME BAKERY.

If you need a pretzel in the case that isn’t a mall pretzel, this is the program. Grocery and retail are already on the soft pretzel wholesale list. You pick the hold that matches how you sell.

3-Day Fresh Retail

The shop bakes fresh daily. Retail partners can run a 3-day shelf when you want the counter energy without a freezer program.

Flash-Frozen Hold

Fully baked, then immediately frozen and vacuum-sealed. Longer hold for accounts that need it. You reheat. We dial that in.

Not Raw Dough

No proofing. No lye boil. No bakery staff in the back of the store. We baked it in Salt Lake City.

Twists, Bombs, Dips

Savory and sweet twists people already know. Pretzel bombs for grab-and-go. House-made dips packed for service.

See current retail flavors on the menu. The distributor page lists 6 savory, 1 sweet, 5 bomb varieties, and 3 house-made dips as the product range. Assortment is confirmed when we open the account — we don’t publish case packs or MOQs here.

HOW RETAIL ORDERS WORK

Same path as the rest of wholesale. Standing orders or on-demand. SLC van or regional freight. Flexible minimums — set with you, not on a public price list.

SLC Metro or Freight

Direct delivery in the Salt Lake City metro. Outside the metro we move product through freight partnerships. Frozen cases can run seven days a week.

One Store or a Region

GROCERY & RETAIL FAQ

Can grocery and retail stores buy Dangerous Pretzel wholesale?

Yes. Grocery and retail are a core wholesale audience. Stock a local favorite in the case. Start on the wholesale page or email info@dangerouspretzel.com.

Do retail partners get fresh pretzels or flash-frozen?

Both paths exist. The shop bakes fresh daily. For retail partners there is a 3-day shelf option. Flash-frozen is available when you need longer holding. That’s the same split on the frozen foodservice page.

What flavors can we stock?

Retail flavors live on the shop menu. The distributor page lists 6 savory, 1 sweet, 5 bomb varieties, and 3 house-made dips as the product range. Wholesale assortment is confirmed when we open the account — we do not publish case packs or MOQs.
