REHEAT FAQ

How does wholesale reheat work?

Partners reheat from frozen. Every pretzel is fully baked in our Salt Lake City kitchen, then immediately flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed. We stay on the process with every account until the pretzel tastes fresh out of the oven — pull-apart texture included.

Do you publish oven temperatures or reheat times?

No. The site does not publish reheat temperatures, times, or frozen shelf-life days. We dial in the best reheating process with every partner so they taste like the shop.

Is this raw dough we have to finish?

No. Fully baked. We do not ship raw dough. There is no proofing, boil, or bakery staff. You reheat. That is the deal.
