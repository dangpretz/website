INTERMOUNTAIN FAQ

Do you wholesale soft pretzels outside Salt Lake City?

Yes. Direct delivery covers the Salt Lake City metro. Outside the metro we move product through freight partnerships — the same regional path on the wholesale and distributor pages.

Is Mountain West a real option, or just Utah?

The distributor inquiry lists Salt Lake City metro, Utah statewide, Mountain West, and national as regions. Direct wholesale is for one business stocking its own service. Distribution is for a network that wants to carry the brand in a region.

Where are the pretzels baked?

In our kitchen at 352 W 600 S, Salt Lake City, UT 84101 — the Post District shop. Wholesale pretzels are fully baked there, then flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed.
