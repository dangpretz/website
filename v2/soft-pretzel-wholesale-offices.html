OFFICE & CAMPUS FAQ

Can offices and campuses buy Dangerous Pretzel wholesale?

Yes. Offices and campuses are a core wholesale audience on the wholesale hub — fuel the team differently, with standing orders and no kitchen build-out. Start with the inquiry form or email info@dangerouspretzel.com.

Do we need a kitchen or bakery staff on campus?

No. Wholesale pretzels arrive fully baked, flash-frozen, and vacuum-sealed. There is no proofing, boil, or bakery staff. You reheat. We dial in that process with every partner. That’s the same story on how wholesale reheat works.

Is office wholesale the same as catering a corporate lunch?

No. Wholesale is standing or on-demand frozen cases for your own service. A one-off event or corporate lunch is catering. Use the wholesale inquiry for an account; use the catering page for a single event.
