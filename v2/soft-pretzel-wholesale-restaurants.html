RESTAURANT & BAR FAQ

Can restaurants and bars buy Dangerous Pretzel wholesale?

Yes. Restaurants and bars are a core wholesale audience. One bar partner put it simply: customers order them on repeat. Start on the wholesale page or email info@dangerouspretzel.com.

Do we need a bakery or extra kitchen staff?

No. Wholesale pretzels arrive fully baked, flash-frozen, and vacuum-sealed. There is no proofing, boil, or bakery staff. You reheat. We dial in that process with every partner.

Is this the same as the Pretzel Program for venues?

No. Restaurant and bar wholesale is back-of-house reheat from frozen cases. The Pretzel Program is a plug-in heated station for venues that want staff to serve, not cook.
