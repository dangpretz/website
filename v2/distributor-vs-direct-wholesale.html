WHICH FORM FAQ

What’s the difference between wholesale and becoming a distributor?

Direct wholesale is for restaurants, bars, offices, retailers, and venues that want to stock Dangerous Pretzel for their own guests. Distribution is for companies with a network who want to carry the brand in a region — Salt Lake City metro, Utah statewide, the Mountain West, or national.

Which form should I use?

Buying for one business: the wholesale inquiry. Covering a territory or retailer list: the distributor inquiry. If you’re not sure, send wholesale and say so — we can point you.

Is the product different?

Both paths start from the same SLC bakery. Wholesale and distributor pages both describe weekly standing orders or on-demand, flexible minimums, SLC metro delivery, regional freight, a 3-day fresh retail shelf, and a flash-frozen option. Assortment is confirmed when the account is set up.
