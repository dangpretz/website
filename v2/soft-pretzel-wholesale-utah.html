UTAH WHOLESALE FAQ

Do you deliver wholesale pretzels in Salt Lake City?

Yes. We offer direct delivery in the Salt Lake City metro. Frozen cases can be scheduled seven days a week.

What about the rest of Utah?

Outside the metro we move product through freight partnerships. That’s the same regional path listed on the wholesale and distributor pages.

Where are the pretzels baked?

In our kitchen at 352 W 600 S, Salt Lake City, UT 84101 — the Post District shop. Wholesale pretzels are fully baked there, then flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed.
