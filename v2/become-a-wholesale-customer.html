CHECKLIST BEFORE YOU APPLY

Same facts as the wholesale hub. Fill these in on the form below — or say them on the call. We do not ask you to guess case packs or MOQs; those are set with the account.

1. Business & Type

Name plus one of the types already on the form: restaurant/bar, office/campus, grocery/retail, event venue, or other.

2. City

Location tells us the path. Salt Lake City metro is direct frozen delivery. Outside the metro we use freight partnerships.

3. What You Want to Serve

Twists, bombs, house-made dips — or “not sure yet.” Retail flavors are on the menu. Assortment is confirmed when we open the account.

4. Hold Path

Flash-frozen foodservice is the default wholesale hold. Fresh retail can run a 3-day shelf. Say which you need — we don’t publish frozen shelf-life days.

START-HERE FAQ

How do I start a wholesale account?

Send the wholesale inquiry form on this page, email info@dangerouspretzel.com, or call (801) 916-0275 / text (385) 888-7603. Tell us the business, the city, and what you are trying to serve. Existing partners order at the wholesale portal.

What should I include in the inquiry?

Business name, contact, email, business type (restaurant/bar, office/campus, grocery/retail, event venue, or other), location/city, and a short note about what you want to serve. Assortment, case packs, and minimums are confirmed when we open the account — they are not on a public list.

Is this the distributor form?

No. This page starts a direct wholesale account — one business stocking its own service. Carrying the brand across a region is the distributor inquiry.
