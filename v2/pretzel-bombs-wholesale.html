PRETZEL BOMBS FAQ

Do you sell pretzel bombs wholesale?

Yes. Pretzel bombs are part of the wholesale program — bite-size pretzels for concessions, taprooms, and grab-and-go. Same dough as the full twists, smaller trouble. Start on the wholesale page or email info@dangerouspretzel.com.

How many bomb varieties are there?

The distributor page lists 5 bomb varieties in the product range, next to 6 savory, 1 sweet, and 3 house-made dips. Retail flavors live on the shop menu. Wholesale assortment is confirmed when we open the account — we do not publish case packs, MOQs, or a wholesale price list.

Are catering bomb trays the same as wholesale bombs?

No. A bomb tray for one event is catering. Wholesale is standing or on-demand frozen cases you reheat in your own service. Same kitchen. Different door. More on catering vs wholesale.
