VS MALL FAQ

Is Dangerous Pretzel a mall pretzel?

No. Dangerous Pretzel Co. is a Salt Lake City shop pretzel sold wholesale to restaurants, bars, offices, grocery, and venues. The wholesale hub already says it: if you want a pretzel that is not a mall pretzel on your menu or in your case, this is the program.

How is wholesale different from a commodity foodservice pretzel?

We bake every pretzel fully in our Salt Lake City kitchen — never raw dough — then flash-freeze and vacuum-seal it. Partners reheat. We stay on that process until it tastes like the shop. Assortment includes savory and sweet twists, pretzel bombs, and house-made dips from the shop menu. We do not publish case packs, MOQs, or prices here.
