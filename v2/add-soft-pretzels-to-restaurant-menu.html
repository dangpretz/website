ADD-TO-MENU FAQ

How do we add Dangerous Pretzel to a restaurant menu?

Send the wholesale inquiry with the restaurant or bar, the city, and what you want to serve. We open the account, confirm assortment, and dial in the reheat with your line so they taste fresh out of the oven. Then you order standing or on-demand through the wholesale portal.

Do we need a bakery or extra kitchen staff?

No. Wholesale pretzels arrive fully baked, flash-frozen, and vacuum-sealed. There is no proofing, boil, or bakery staff. You reheat. We stay on that process. The site does not publish oven temperatures, times, or frozen shelf-life days. That’s the same story on how wholesale reheat works.

Which flavors can we put on the menu?

Retail flavors live on the shop menu — savory and sweet twists, pretzel bombs, and house-made dips. Wholesale assortment is confirmed when we set up the account. We do not publish case packs, MOQs, or a wholesale price list on this page.
