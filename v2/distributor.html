DISTRIBUTOR FAQ

Should I become a distributor or just carry Dangerous Pretzel?

If you have a network and want to carry the brand in a region, use the distributor inquiry. If you are a restaurant, bar, office, or retailer stocking pretzels for your own guests, that is direct wholesale — not this form.

Which form should I use?

Covering a territory or retailer list: the distributor inquiry on this page. Buying for one business: the wholesale inquiry. If you are not sure, send wholesale and say so — we can point you.

What regions can I apply for?

The distributor form lists Salt Lake City metro, Utah statewide, Mountain West, and national. Those are application regions — not a published coverage map or territory guarantee.

Is the product different for distributors?

Both paths start from the same SLC bakery. This page already lists weekly standing orders or on-demand, flexible minimums, SLC metro delivery, regional freight, a 3-day fresh retail shelf, and a flash-frozen option. Assortment is confirmed when the account is set up.
