Do other distributors carry Dangerous Pretzel for a university concession?

Dangerous Pretzel Co. is available through US Foods in Northern Utah and Boise. If you buy through another distributor, tell us which one on the wholesale inquiry and we'll look into it.

How does reheat work for a concession stand?

Partners reheat from frozen. Every pretzel is fully baked in the Salt Lake City kitchen, then flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed. The site does not publish oven temperatures, times, or frozen shelf-life days. See how wholesale reheat works.

How do I request a sample drop-off for a college or university concession?

Request a sample drop-off on the wholesale inquiry. Paul or Emery from the team can drop off samples. Ask through the wholesale inquiry, info@dangerouspretzel.com, or (801) 916-0275.

Who should contact you about a university arena or stadium concession?

The university dining or athletics concessions manager, or the concessionaire who runs the basketball arena, football stadium, or campus event stand. Send the wholesale inquiry with the school or venue, a contact name, and what you want to serve. Email info@dangerouspretzel.com or call (801) 916-0275.
