THE REHEAT STORY

This is not frozen dough you have to finish. The shop side of Dangerous is hand-rolled and baked to order. The wholesale side uses that same bake, then locks it in so a restaurant, bar, office, or venue can serve it without a pretzel program in the back.

1. Baked In-House

Fully baked in Salt Lake City. Never a “finish it yourself” kit.

2. Flash-Frozen & Sealed

Immediately frozen and vacuum-sealed so flavor and pull-apart texture survive the trip.

3. Delivered Frozen

Frozen cases on your schedule, including seven-day delivery. We handle the logistics.

4. You Reheat

We dial in the best reheating process with every partner. That’s the deal.

Full buyer path — who it’s for, SLC vs freight, how to start — lives on the soft pretzel wholesale hub. We don’t publish frozen shelf-life days or case packs on the site.

FRESH VS FLASH-FROZEN

Two holds. Same bakery. Pick the one that matches how you serve.

Shop / Fresh Retail

The shop bakes fresh daily. For retail partners there is a 3-day shelf option when you want a local favorite in the case without a freezer program.

Foodservice Frozen

Flash-frozen for restaurants, bars, offices, campuses, grocery that needs longer hold, and event venues. Standing orders or on-demand.

FROZEN PRETZEL FAQ

Are wholesale pretzels raw dough or fully baked?

Fully baked. We do not ship raw dough. Pretzels are baked in-house in Salt Lake City, then immediately flash-frozen and vacuum-sealed.

How does the reheat work?

Partners reheat from frozen. We stay on the process with every account until the pretzel tastes fresh out of the oven — pull-apart texture included.

Do you only sell flash-frozen, or is there a fresh option?

The shop bakes fresh daily. For retail partners there is a 3-day shelf option. Flash-frozen is the foodservice path for accounts that need longer holding.
