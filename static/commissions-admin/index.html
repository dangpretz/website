SKU Mapping

Every item name that's actually shown up on a confirmed wholesale delivery, whether from Square or a manual entry. Connect it to a SKU to make it priceable in the Sales CRM's Pricing view — leave it blank for anything that isn't a commissioned product (drinks, catering boxes, one-offs). New item names show up here automatically as new orders come in.

Commission Tier Schedules

One shared price-to-commission ladder per SKU — the same schedule applies no matter which customer is buying. Each customer's own price (set in the Sales CRM's Pricing view) is looked up against this ladder to get their commission %. Edits here never change commissions already locked in by Compute. A SKU only becomes priceable once it has a ladder; use “Visible to reps” to keep a priceable SKU out of the reps’ Pricing view.
